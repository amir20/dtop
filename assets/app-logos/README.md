# App logos

Generated by `scripts/import-dozzle-icons.py` from Dozzle's app icons (Dozzle d4fa7a68); don't edit by hand. Each icon is fitted to 32x32 and saved as a lossless PNG.

Most icons come from [homarr-labs/dashboard-icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons), which is MIT licensed. `magpie` comes from [Magpie's official icon](https://github.com/yetone/magpie), also MIT licensed. The logos themselves remain the property of their respective projects and are used here to identify them.
